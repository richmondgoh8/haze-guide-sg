How to use PM2.5 and PSI when planning your day.

DECIDING NOW

PM2.5 for the next hour

1 hour of measured particles

These tiny particles can get deep into your lungs. The 1-hour reading tells you how much was in the air over the past hour. It can change quickly, so check again before heading out.

THE LONGER VIEW

24-hour PSI overview

24 hours not tomorrow’s forecast

Singapore’s 24-hour PSI is an air-quality index based on several pollutants. The reading shown here describes recent conditions. For tomorrow’s outing, check NEA’s forecast and advisory.

COMPARE LIKE FOR LIKE

Why other apps differ

AQI ≠ PSI different index, different number

Countries calculate “AQI” differently. This site uses NEA’s original Singapore readings and bands. Compare the pollutant, averaging period and source before comparing two numbers.
