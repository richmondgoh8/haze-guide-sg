Which mask, at which haze level?

Use the 1-hour PM2.5 reading for immediate plans. These practical choices follow MOH’s activity guidance; the value notes are our editorial suggestions, not product endorsements.

Mask choices for all four haze levels
Normal · 0–55 µg/m³

Keep your normal plans

Ordinary activities can continue. A respirator is generally unnecessary for a short commute or indoors.

Value move: keep a small supply ready rather than wearing a new mask routinely for normal air.

Elevated · 56–150 µg/m³

Ease off strenuous exercise

Healthy adults should reduce strenuous outdoor activity; higher-risk people should avoid it.

Value move: shorten or move the workout indoors. A higher-rated mask is not a reason to push through.

High · 151–250 µg/m³

Skip strenuous outdoor activity

Higher-risk people should avoid all outdoor activity. If exposure is unavoidable, a well-fitting N95 or equivalent can reduce particles inhaled; this is also relevant for prolonged exposure.

Value move: use a genuine, well-fitting N95 / KN95 / KF94 / FFP2 you can afford.

Very high · ≥251 µg/m³

Minimise time outdoors

Higher-risk people should avoid outdoor activity. For unavoidable time outside, consider a well-fitting N95 or equivalent.

Value move: prioritise less exposure and a reliable seal. MOH does not require an N99 or P100 upgrade.
