Safe use & who needs extra care

Cover your nose and mouth, mould the nosepiece, and follow the maker’s seal-check instructions. Gaps and facial hair at the seal reduce protection.

Follow the maker’s use and replacement instructions. Replace a mask when damaged, dirty, damp or difficult to breathe through; do not wash disposable respirators. Do not share them. For children, adult masks may not seal: prioritise staying indoors and ask a healthcare professional about suitable protection. Older adults, pregnant people and people with severe heart or lung problems who have difficulty breathing should consult a doctor about respirator use. If you feel unwell, seek medical advice.
