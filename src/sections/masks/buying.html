Genuine mask? Check your type.

A listed model can still be copied. Buy through a traceable seller; a good seal still matters. Check the pack’s batch details and expiry too.

N95

NIOSH · US

  • Find the manufacturer, model and TC approval number.
  • Match them in the official list—not just a seller’s certificate photo.

KN95

GB 2626 · China

  • Check the maker, model and stated standard. Current production: GB 2626-2019.
  • Ask for matching test evidence. Verify doubtful reports with the issuing lab through its own website.

The standard number is a claim, not an authenticity code.

KF94

MFDS · Korea

  • Match the exact product, manufacturer, KF94 grade and size in Korea’s approval list.
  • Use the Korean product name from the pack. Browser translation helps with the menus.

Different routes, not one universal checker. NIOSH checks don’t verify KN95, KF94 or FFP2 claims.

Before you pay

  • Seller first. Use an established retailer or a seller the manufacturer confirms. A marketplace badge isn’t a guarantee.

  • Pay in-app. Use protected checkout. Avoid direct transfers, seller-sent payment links and app downloads.

  • Compare, don’t stockpile. Try a small pack for fit. Compare retailer prices and Price Kaki ↗ where listed; prices and coverage can vary.
