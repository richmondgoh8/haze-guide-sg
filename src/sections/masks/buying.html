Buying online? Spot fake masks & scams

Seller first

Choose an established pharmacy or retailer. Unknown seller? Skip the gamble.

N95? Check proof

Match the maker, model and TC approval number in the NIOSH list ↗. Check expiry too.

Pay in-app

Use the platform’s protected checkout. Avoid direct transfers, seller-sent payment links and app downloads.

A copied approval number isn’t proof of authenticity. NIOSH checks apply to N95 claims—not KN95, KF94 or FFP2.

Unsure about a deal? Call ScamShield 1799. Already paid a scammer? Contact your bank immediately and report to police.
