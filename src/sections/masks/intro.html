04 / A PRACTICAL MASK GUIDE

Mask or no mask?

MOH includes N95, KN95, KF94 and FFP2 as options for haze particles. A genuine, well-fitting model from these groups is a practical starting point when a respirator is needed.

  • ✓A short commute? MOH says a respirator is generally unnecessary for routine commuting or indoors. Follow the current band and your health needs.
  • ✓Check the seal every time. Cover your nose and mouth fully, mould the nosepiece, and follow the maker’s seal-check instructions. Gaps and facial hair at the seal reduce protection.
  • ✓Buy a small pack first. Compare the delivered price per mask after finding a certified model that seals comfortably. Surgical fluid resistance adds no particle-filtration benefit for haze.
