Lab rating ≠ protection on your face. These standards use different tests. The percentages are minimum filter results, not a guarantee of how much exposure you avoid. Fit, authenticity and time outside matter.

Best value? A genuine model that seals comfortably, at a sustainable price per mask. Buy a small pack first. A higher rating with a poor seal isn’t an upgrade.
