ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Useful advice. Clear limits.

Independent, open source

Air, Actually is maintained by GitHub user richmondgoh8. It is not affiliated with NEA or MOH. Government readings remain attributed to their original providers; explanations and household examples are our editorial interpretation.

Report an error or suggest a correction. Please include the page and source you’re referring to. There are no affiliate links or paid product rankings on this site.

Sources, not medical credentials

Guidance is checked against the official sources linked beside each guide. The latest content check was 17 Oct 2026; this is separate from the timestamps on live readings. This is general information, not personalised medical advice or a claim of clinical review. Follow current MOH guidance and your clinician’s advice for your health needs.

Regional outdoor readings cannot measure the air inside your home. Missing or old readings do not mean the air is safe. Readings-specific recommendations are withheld when data is unavailable or stale.
